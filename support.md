## Need help?

ScentPass is a personal fragrance journal — build your olfactory passport, track the perfumes and colognes you own or want to try, explore a rich fragrance catalog, and discover new scents based on your taste.

If you have a question, found a bug, or want to share feedback, we'd love to hear from you.

We usually reply within 2–3 business days.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I create an account?**
You can use ScentPass in guest mode without an account, or sign in with your email and password or with Google to sync your collection across devices.

**How do I add a fragrance to my collection?**
Search the catalog, open a fragrance, and add it to your collection as owned, tried, or on your wishlist. You can add your own notes and ratings to each scent.

**How are the recommendations generated?**
Suggestions are based on the fragrance families and notes you already like, plus the scents you save and interact with in the app.

**How do I delete my account and data?**
See our [Account Deletion](https://ganbarelabs.github.io/scentpass-legal/account-deletion) page. In short: email us from your account's email address with the subject "Delete my account" and we'll permanently remove your data within 30 days.
