## Need help?

ScentPass is a personal fragrance journal — build your olfactory passport, track the perfumes and colognes you own or want to try, explore a rich fragrance catalog, review the scents you know, and discover new ones based on your taste.

If you have a question, found a bug, or want to share feedback, we'd love to hear from you.

**Email:** scentpass.support@gmail.com

We usually reply within 2–3 business days.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I create an account?**
You can use ScentPass in guest mode without an account, or sign in with your email and password, Google, or Apple to sync your collection across devices.

**How do I add a fragrance to my collection?**
Search the catalog, open a fragrance, and add it to your collection as owned, tried, or on your wishlist.

**How do I review a fragrance?**
Open a fragrance and publish your review. You can share a quick impression or rate longevity, sillage, season, and more, with or without text. You need an account to publish, and you can edit or delete your review at any time.

**Who can see my reviews?**
Reviews are public: other users see the text together with your username and avatar. Your ratings also count towards the community scores of each fragrance.

**How are reviews moderated?**
Every review is checked automatically before it is published, and it must follow the community guidelines in our [Terms & Conditions](https://ganbarelabs.github.io/scentpass-legal/terms-and-conditions). Reviews that break them are removed, and repeat offenders lose the ability to publish.

**How do I report a review or a user?**
Use the report option on any review in the app. Reported reviews are checked by our team, and reviews with several reports are hidden until then. You can also email us at scentpass.support@gmail.com with the fragrance and the username.

**How are the recommendations generated?**
Suggestions are based on the fragrance families and notes you already like, plus the scents you save and interact with in the app.

**How do I delete my account and data?**
Go to Settings in the app and choose "Delete account". You can also follow the steps on our [Account Deletion](https://ganbarelabs.github.io/scentpass-legal/account-deletion) page. The text of your reviews and your username are deleted; your ratings remain only as anonymous contributions to the community scores.
